# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### How they interact with other roles
- **Collaborate with Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and feature specifications
- **Report to Project Managers** on progress, blockers, and estimates
- **Work with QA/Testing Personnel** to define testability and resolve defects
- **Consult with Technical Leads/Architects** on design decisions and technical risks
- **Coordinate with Scrum Masters** on sprint ceremonies and impediment removal

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### How they interact with other roles
- **Engage with Stakeholders & Business Sponsors** to gather requirements and confirm priorities
- **Brief Project Managers** on scope and success metrics
- **Define acceptance criteria with Developers** and QA/Testing Personnel
- **Consult with Technical Leads/Architects** on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- **Participate in sprint planning with Scrum Masters** to refine backlog priorities

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### How they interact with other roles
- **Report to Stakeholders & Business Sponsors** on project status, risks, and milestones
- **Coordinate with Developers, QA/Testing Personnel, and Technical Leads** on schedule and resource planning
- **Work with Scrum Masters** to facilitate ceremonies and manage workflow
- **Escalate blockers and risks** involving Security & Compliance Officer when needed
- **Enable communication** between all roles to ensure alignment

---

## QA/Testing Personnel

### Role Summary
QA and Testing specialists ensure that features meet acceptance criteria, quality standards, and user expectations before release. They work closely with developers and product managers to validate functionality, performance, and user experience.

### Responsibilities
- Execute manual and automated test plans based on acceptance criteria
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate with developers on test strategy and edge cases
- Participate in sprint planning to understand scope and testability
- Validate fixes and retest before sign-off
- Assist in creating test documentation and scenarios

### Goals
- Catch quality issues early and reduce production defects
- Enable confident releases with minimal rework
- Improve test coverage and automation over time

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test case reviews with developers
- Defect reports and tracking in issue management systems
- QA sign-off on acceptance criteria before release

### How they interact with other roles
- **Work with Developers** to clarify test cases, understand technical implementation, and validate fixes
- **Align with Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and feature priorities
- **Report to Project Managers** on test progress and blocking issues
- **Collaborate with Technical Leads/Architects** on testing strategy for complex components
- **Support Scrum Masters** in sprint ceremonies for test scope planning
- **Coordinate with Security & Compliance Officer** on security and compliance testing requirements

---

## Stakeholders & Business Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Sponsors provide business context, approvals, and inputs that shape project direction. They represent customer needs, business priorities, and organizational constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project initiation and key milestones
- Participate in kickoff and review meetings
- Escalate business-critical blockers
- Review progress and provide feedback on priorities
- Champion the project and support resource allocation

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery and business strategy
- Support rapid escalation and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and stakeholder alignment meetings
- Weekly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gates and approval reviews
- Escalation paths for business-impacting issues

### How they interact with other roles
- **Provide direction to Product Managers** on business priorities and success metrics
- **Receive status from Project Managers** on project progress and risks
- **Attend demos and reviews** with Developers and QA/Testing Personnel to validate deliverables
- **Support decision-making** involving Technical Leads/Architects on strategic technology choices
- **Escalate to sponsors** when Security & Compliance Officer identifies business-impacting constraints
- **Enable resource and organizational support** for project success

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide the team on architectural decisions, technical strategy, and risk mitigation. They mentor developers, review designs, and ensure the solution is scalable and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review technical architecture and design patterns
- Mentor developers on best practices and code quality
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Make or advise on critical technology decisions
- Review critical pull requests and technical approaches
- Support capacity and feasibility planning during project planning

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future risk
- Build team capability and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews on critical components
- Project planning for technical feasibility and estimates
- Risk register updates for technical dependencies

### How they interact with other roles
- **Mentor and review work** with Developers on technical implementation
- **Advise Product Managers** on technical feasibility of features
- **Inform Project Managers** on technical risks and dependencies
- **Collaborate with QA/Testing Personnel** on testing strategy for complex systems
- **Partner with Security & Compliance Officer** to ensure security architecture and compliance
- **Support Scrum Masters** in technical retrospectives and process improvements

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on Agile practices. They enable the team to work collaboratively and continuously improve their delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers to team progress
- Coach team members on Agile principles and practices
- Maintain sprint board and manage workflow
- Identify process improvements and drive team adoption
- Shield the team from external distractions

### Goals
- Enable consistent, sustainable team velocity
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce cycle time and improve delivery predictability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Retrospective action items and follow-up
- Escalation of blockers to Project Managers

### How they interact with other roles
- **Facilitate collaboration** among Developers, QA/Testing Personnel, and Technical Leads
- **Support Product Managers** in backlog refinement and sprint planning
- **Work with Project Managers** to surface risks and dependencies
- **Coach all roles** on Agile practices and team collaboration
- **Escalate organizational impediments** to Project Managers and Stakeholders
- **Support continuous improvement** across the team

---

## Security & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security, compliance, and regulatory requirements. They work across the team to integrate security practices, conduct reviews, and manage compliance risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define and enforce security and compliance requirements for projects
- Review designs, code, and deployments for security vulnerabilities
- Conduct security scanning and compliance audits
- Manage incident response and security escalation
- Ensure regulatory and compliance obligations are met
- Provide guidance on security best practices

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Build a security-aware culture across teams
- Maintain organizational trust and regulatory standing

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat modeling sessions
- Compliance audits and assessment reports
- Incident response coordination
- Security training and awareness communications

### How they interact with other roles
- **Review code and designs** with Developers and Technical Leads/Architects
- **Define acceptance criteria** with Product Managers for security features
- **Report risks and status** to Project Managers and Stakeholders
- **Collaborate with QA/Testing Personnel** on security and compliance testing
- **Partner with Scrum Masters** to integrate security into team processes
- **Provide guidance** to all roles on security and compliance requirements
